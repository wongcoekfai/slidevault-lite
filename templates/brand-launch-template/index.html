Free brand launch presentation template for revealing a new identity. Vision, visual system, collection highlights and rollout timeline slides.

For brand teams unveiling a new or refreshed identity. Includes vision and rationale, visual system overview, application highlights and a phased rollout timeline for internal and external audiences.

Works with PowerPoint and Google Slides. Fully editable - change colors, fonts, text and images freely. Free for personal and commercial use. No signup required.

What is in the Brand Launch Template

This deck ships with 12 slides on a 16:9 canvas.   • Visual language
  • Who we design for
  • Channels & retail

Marketing decks usually carry two kinds of content, the story and the numbers, and they read badly when mixed. Keep the campaign narrative on one set of slides and the KPI evidence on another, and put the channel result before the explanation of how you got it. The layouts here keep those two tracks separate by default.

How to customize it in five minutes

  1. Download the .pptx and open it in PowerPoint, or upload it to Google Slides with File → Import slides. Layouts and theme colours come through on both.
  2. Replace the placeholder copy. Every headline, caption and number is a normal text box, so you can restyle a single slide without breaking the rest of the deck.
  3. Apply your brand colours from Design → Variants → Colors. The deck is built on a handful of theme colours, so one click recolours every slide consistently.
  4. Swap the images with right-click → Change Picture and keep the crop. Placeholders are sized for 16:9, so replacements fill the frame without shifting the layout.
  5. Delete the slides you do not need, duplicate a layout for extra content, then export to PDF if you are sending the deck ahead of the meeting.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Brand Launch Template really free?

Yes. Download the .pptx directly, with no signup, no email and no watermark. It is free for personal and commercial use, including client work. The only restriction is that you cannot resell or redistribute the template file itself.

Does the Brand Launch Template work in Google Slides?

Yes. Upload the .pptx to Google Drive and open it with Google Slides; layouts, fonts and colours stay editable. If the deck uses a typeface Google does not host, swap it for a Google font from the toolbar and the layout holds.

Can I use the Brand Launch Template for client work?

Yes. Commercial use is allowed, and you can change the copy, colours and imagery freely. What you may not do is sell the file as a template product or bundle it into a paid template pack.

Do I need design skills to edit the Brand Launch Template?

No. Every slide is built from standard shapes and text boxes on a 16:9 canvas, so editing means typing over text and picking theme colours. If you can use PowerPoint or Google Slides at all, you can finish this deck.
