About Slide Vault

What we do

Slide Vault is a small library of free, professionally designed presentation templates for PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote. Every template is fully editable, works offline, and requires no signup to download.

We focus on the templates people actually need at work: business presentations, pitch decks, marketing and annual reports, sales proposals, business plans and training decks.

Why free?

The site is supported by advertising and affiliate recommendations, which keeps every template free for everyone. When we recommend a paid product (such as a premium template marketplace or a design tool), the link may be an affiliate link — it costs you nothing extra and helps us keep the free templates coming.

Our promise

  • No signup walls. Click download, get the file.
  • No hidden watermarks. Templates are clean and ready to edit.
  • Real content. Each template ships with a matching guide on our blog that explains how to use it well.

Who makes it

Slide Vault is built and maintained by a small team of designers and business writers. We use the same templates ourselves in consulting and startup work — everything published here has survived a real meeting first.
