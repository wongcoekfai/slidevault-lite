Template License

In plain language: our templates are free for personal and commercial use, and you never have to credit us. There is exactly one thing you cannot do with them.

What you can do (free, forever)

  • Download and use every template for personal, academic and commercial work — including decks you deliver to clients, decks you present internally, and course materials you teach from.
  • Edit anything: text, colors, fonts, images, charts, slide order, adding and deleting slides. The templates are a starting point, not a locked design.
  • No attribution required. You do not have to credit Slide Vault in the deck or anywhere else. A mention is always appreciated, never owed.
  • No signup, no watermark, no paywall — the files on the download page are the full files.

The one thing you cannot do

  • Resell or redistribute the template files themselves. You may not upload our .pptx files to stock/template marketplaces, bundle them into a paid template pack, or offer them as your own templates, whether for money or as a lead magnet.
  • Use our preview images and cover art to market another template product. Screenshots of a finished deck you built from our template are fine.

Everything you create with a template — your content, your branding, your data — is yours. Building client decks, reports or teaching material on top of our templates and delivering the result is exactly what they are for.

Fonts

Templates are built with widely available system typefaces (Calibri, Arial and similar), so nothing needs to be installed or purchased. If you swap in a third-party font of your own, that font's own license applies to your file. Opening the deck in Google Slides may substitute Calibri with Carlito — a metric-compatible twin — so line breaks and layouts stay intact; see the font troubleshooting guide.

No warranty

Templates are provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind. Slide Vault is not liable for how the decks are used or for any outcomes of presentations built with them. Check the numbers in every chart before you present — they are sample data on purpose.

Questions

If a use case is not covered here — a course you sell, a workshop kit, a nonprofit drive — just ask via the contact page. The answer is usually yes.
