Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

We collect as little as possible. We use Google advertising (which sets cookies) to keep the templates free — you can opt out anytime. We never sell your email address.

What we collect

Email address (only if you subscribe)

If you fill in the subscribe form, we store your email address and the time you subscribed. It is used for one thing only: sending you a short note when new templates are published. Submissions are stored in a database inside our own Cloudflare account — no third-party email service sees them.

Anonymous download counts

When you download a template, we count how many times each file was downloaded. We do not store your IP address or any identifier alongside it.

Advertising and cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies — including the DoubleClick cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. See the next section for your opt-out choices.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your email address.
  • We never send marketing email other than new-template announcements.

Advertising choices (opt-out)

You can opt out of personalized advertising in several ways:

  • Google Ads Settings: google.com/settings/ads
  • AboutAds (US): aboutads.info/choices
  • YourOnlineChoices (EU): youronlinechoices.com

Opting out does not remove ads — it makes them less personalized. For more detail, see Google's advertising privacy policy.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like every web server, Cloudflare processes technical request data (IP address, user agent) to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or just contact us and we will remove you promptly.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@slidevaulte.com.
