Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

We collect as little as possible. No ads, no trackers, no data sales - ever.

What we collect

Email address (only if you subscribe)

If you fill in the subscribe form, we store your email address and the time you subscribed. It is used for one thing only: sending you a short note when new templates are published. Submissions are stored in a database inside our own Cloudflare account - no third-party email service sees them.

Anonymous download counts

When you download a template, we count how many times each file was downloaded. We do not store your IP address or any identifier alongside it.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your email address.
  • We never send marketing email other than new-template announcements.
  • We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Hosting and logs

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like every web server, Cloudflare processes technical request data (IP address, user agent) to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under Cloudflare's own privacy policy. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not fingerprint visitors.

Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or just contact us and we will remove you promptly.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@slidevaulte.com.
