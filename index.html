Frequently Asked Questions

Are these templates really free?

Yes — every template on Slide Vault is 100% free. No signup, no email wall, no hidden fees. Download and go.

Can I use them for commercial purposes?

Yes. Use them for client work, company decks, pitches, teaching — personal and commercial use is allowed. You may not resell or redistribute the templates themselves as templates.

Do they work with Google Slides?

Yes. Upload the .pptx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Slides — layouts, fonts and colors carry over and stay fully editable.

We publish new decks regularly. Subscribe below and you'll get one short email whenever a new template drops — nothing else.
