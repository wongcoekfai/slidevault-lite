How it works

  1. Add your deck. Click the box (or drag & drop) and pick the .pptx presentation you want to split.
  2. Pick a mode. One file per slide, or fixed-size parts (e.g. every 10 slides) for sharing long decks in chunks.
  3. Split & download. Press Split, wait a few seconds, and download a ZIP with all the parts.

Why split decks in the browser?

Typical online splitters upload your presentation to a server, process it there, and hand it back — a privacy risk for internal reports, client work or anything confidential. This tool runs directly on your device: nothing is transmitted, there is no file queue, no watermark and no registration. Each output file keeps the deck's original slide masters, layouts and theme, so every slide looks exactly as it did in the source deck.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit. The site is supported by ads.

How do I extract a single slide from a big deck?

Choose One file per slide and split — then just keep the slide file you need and delete the rest. Every slide keeps its original design.

Why are the parts almost as big as the original?

Each part carries the deck's shared slide masters and theme media so the design stays intact — in photo-heavy decks that media is most of the file size. Run the parts through our Compress PPT tool to shrink them.

Can I split .ppt or Google Slides files?

Only .pptx is supported. Google Slides: use File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) first. Legacy .ppt files need a quick save-as .pptx in PowerPoint.

Do you keep a copy of my presentation?

No. The file is read into your browser's memory, split there, and handed straight back to you as a ZIP download. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
