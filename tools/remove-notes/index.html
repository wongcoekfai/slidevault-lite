    How it works

    1. Add your decks. Click the box (or drag & drop) and pick one or several .pptx presentations.
    2. Strip the notes. Press the button — every notes page is removed from the file structure, not just hidden.
    3. Download. Grab the cleaned file, or a ZIP when you dropped in several decks at once.

    Why do this in the browser?

    Speaker notes are easy to forget: rehearsal cues, salary numbers, half-written jokes, feedback from colleagues. Cloud-based converters mean uploading those notes to someone else's server before you can clean them. This tool works directly on your device — the deck is unpacked in your browser's memory, the notes parts are removed from the file, and the result is handed straight back to you. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged.

    The tool removes the notes content itself, not just the view: notes pages are deleted from the package, so the text cannot reappear through "show notes" or a different viewer. Slide visuals, animations and layouts are untouched.

    FAQ

    Does removing notes change the slides?

    No. Only the notes pages are removed. Slide layouts, fonts, colours, animations and media are untouched, and the deck still opens normally in PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote.

    Why should I remove speaker notes before sharing?

    Notes often contain internal remarks, pricing logic, names and half-finished thoughts. Stripping them avoids accidentally sharing something private when you send the deck to a client, student or conference organiser.

    Can I clean several files at once?

    Yes. Drop in as many .pptx files as you like — you get back a single ZIP with one cleaned file per deck.

    Is the original file modified?

    No. The file is read into browser memory and a new, cleaned copy is created for download. Your original stays exactly as it is on disk.

    Does it work with .ppt or Google Slides files?

    Only .pptx is supported. In Google Slides use File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) first; legacy .ppt files need a quick save-as .pptx in PowerPoint.
