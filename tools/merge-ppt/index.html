    How it works

    1. Add files. Click the box (or drag & drop) and pick the .pptx presentations you want to combine.
    2. Set the order. Slides are merged in list order — use the arrows to move files up or down.
    3. Merge & download. Hit the merge button, wait a few seconds, and download your combined deck.

    Why merge decks in the browser?

    Most online PPT mergers upload your presentation to a server, process it there, and hand it back — a privacy risk for internal reports, client work or anything confidential. This tool uses modern in-browser technology to merge presentations directly on your device: nothing is transmitted, there is no file queue, no watermark and no registration. It also imports each file's slide masters, so slides from different decks keep their original fonts, colors and backgrounds instead of being restyled.

    FAQ

    Is it really free?

    Yes — no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit. The site is supported by ads.

    Will slides from different files keep their design?

    Yes. Each file's slide master and layouts are carried over, so a slide from deck B still looks like it did in deck B after merging.

    Can I merge .ppt or Google Slides files?

    Only .pptx is supported. Google Slides: use File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) first. Legacy .ppt files need a quick save-as .pptx in PowerPoint.

    Is there a file size limit?

    Practically, your device's memory is the limit. Decks up to ~100 MB total merge comfortably on most computers; very large files on old phones may be slow.

    Do you keep a copy of my presentation?

    No. Files are read into your browser's memory, merged there, and handed straight back to you as a download. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
