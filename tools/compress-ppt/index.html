    How it works

    1. Add your file. Click the box (or drag & drop) and pick the .pptx presentation to shrink.
    2. Pick a level. Basic keeps near-original quality; Strong squeezes out every byte for email limits.
    3. Compress & download. Wait a few seconds and download the smaller file — same slides, same design.

    How does it make files smaller?

    Most of a heavy deck's size comes from images: photos shot on modern phones are far larger than any slide needs, and screenshots pasted as PNG can be 10× bigger than necessary. This tool scales oversized images down to a sensible resolution, re-encodes them efficiently, converts needlessly-large PNGs to JPEG when they don't contain transparency, and repacks the whole file with maximum compression. Text, charts, colors, animations and every slide's design stay untouched.

    FAQ

    Is it really free?

    Yes — no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit. The site is supported by ads.

    How much smaller will my file get?

    Photo-heavy decks typically shrink by 40–90%. Even decks with no images get smaller, because the file is repacked with maximum compression.

    Will the deck look the same?

    Visually the difference is barely noticeable. Only oversized images are scaled down and re-encoded at high quality; everything else is byte-for-byte identical.

    Can I compress .ppt or Google Slides files?

    Only .pptx is supported. Google Slides: use File → Download → Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) first. Legacy .ppt files need a quick save-as .pptx in PowerPoint.

    Do you keep a copy of my presentation?

    No. Files are read into your browser's memory, compressed there, and handed straight back as a download. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
