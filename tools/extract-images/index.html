How it works

  1. Add your deck. Click the box (or drag & drop) and pick the .pptx presentation.
  2. Choose what to keep. Keep EMF/WMF clipart and SVG icons included, or uncheck the box to get photos only.
  3. Extract & download. Press Extract images — you get one ZIP with every image at its original quality.

Why extract images in the browser?

PowerPoint buries every picture inside the file — dragging images out one by one is slow and often re-compresses them. This tool opens the deck's package directly and hands you every embedded image at its original quality, untouched and un-resized. It runs directly on your device: nothing is transmitted, there is no file queue, no watermark and no registration. Perfect for reusing brand assets, recovering a lost source photo, or harvesting icons from a template you bought.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes — no sign-up, no watermark, no daily limit. The site is supported by ads.

What image formats are extracted?

All raster images stored in the deck: PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, WebP and TIFF — plus SVG vectors. EMF/WMF Windows clipart is included by default; uncheck the box if you only want photos.

Why does my deck report zero images?

Some presentations draw everything with vector shapes, charts or SmartArt instead of embedding image files — there is simply no image inside to extract. Try a different deck to confirm the tool works.

Are the images re-compressed or renamed?

No. Every file is copied out byte-for-byte at its original quality, keeping its original name (image1.png, image2.jpeg, …).

Do you keep a copy of my presentation?

No. The file is read into your browser's memory, unpacked there, and handed straight back to you as a ZIP download. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
